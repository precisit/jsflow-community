# Code of conduct

This is a place for developers to help each other. We expect everyone here to:

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new;
- stay on the topic of jsflow and what people build with it;
- disagree with ideas, not people; no harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks;
- share only what is yours to share, and never anyone's secrets or personal data.

We may edit or remove posts, and block people, who do not follow this. To report a problem, email support@jsflow.com;
it goes to the people who run this repository (Precisit AB).
