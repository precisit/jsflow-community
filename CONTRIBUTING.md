# Contributing

The most useful things you can do here:

1. **Report a bug** with the issue form. A tiny page that fails is worth more than a long description. Include the SDK
   version, the browser and OS, the exact error (with its code), and the time in UTC.
2. **Ask and answer questions** in Discussions. Other developers read them too.
3. **Suggest features** in Discussions, category Ideas, and say what you are trying to build: that is what we decide from.
4. **Tell us about an example that is wrong or missing** in an issue.

What we do not take here:

- **Pull requests.** This repository is generated from our main repository with every release; a change made here would be
  overwritten. Describe the change in an issue and we make it at the source (and credit you in the release notes if you like).
- **Security reports.** See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not post them publicly.
- **Support requests that need account details.** Email support@jsflow.com (Pro and Scale plans) instead of posting them here.

By posting a suggestion or an example here you agree that we may use it, with credit if you wish, under the MIT licence of this
repository.
